Field manual

The job

You are one Ash Vanguard operator in a procedurally built exclusion zone. Walls block sight and bullets. Crates do too, until they break. Bushes hide a still, quiet body. Gunshots and sprinting footsteps carry through walls.

Skirmish

Two minutes. Eight confirmed downs ends it early. Your deaths go on the Kestrel board. At the buzzer, more kills than deaths holds the field.

Extraction

One life. Find the teal pad — the arrow points even when you cannot see it — and hold inside the ring for five seconds. Hostiles do not come back.

Keyboard and mouse

  • WASD or arrows move
  • Mouse aims, click or F fires
  • IJKL aims without a mouse
  • R reload · Q ability · Shift sprint
  • Esc pause

Touch and gamepad

  • Left stick moves, right stick aims and fires
  • RUN, the amber ability, and reload sit beside the sticks
  • Gamepad: sticks, RT fire, A ability, X reload, B sprint, Start pause

Pickups are ammo, medkits, and armor plates. Seeds rebuild the same compound. Progress stays in this browser.
